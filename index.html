Questions

Before you call

Quick answers to what clients ask most. Still unsure? Send us a request and we will talk it through.

What does ELV mean?

ELV stands for Extra-Low Voltage. It covers the low-power systems in a building such as CCTV, access control, alarms, intercoms, data cabling and networks.

How do I get a quotation?

Use the quote request button below to open our Google Form. We will follow up to arrange a site survey where one is needed, then send a quotation.

Can you work with a system I already have?

Yes, existing equipment can often be extended or integrated. A site survey tells us what can stay and what should be replaced.

Can I watch my cameras and see my door from my phone?

Yes. Modern CCTV and video door phone systems can be set up for remote viewing and two-way calls from a phone. Tell us what you want to see and we will plan it.

Do you offer maintenance after installation?

Yes. We maintain the systems we install so they keep working long after handover. Ask us about a maintenance arrangement when you request a quote.

Do you work with homes as well as businesses?

Yes. We serve homes, offices, commercial facilities and institutions.
